Our Services

Four Circles of Supportive Care

A unified ecosystem of complementary treatments. No need to look up disconnected services across multiple departments.

Circle 1 — Movement & Recovery

Tai Chi, Yoga, & Cancer Rehabilitation

Tailored physical therapies designed for fatigue, neuropathy, balance, strength, and range of motion. Classes are beginner-friendly and adaptable to all levels of mobility.

Circle 2 — Nutrition

Appetite & Dietary Counseling

Oncology dietitian support to manage nausea, taste changes, and hydration needs, helping you maintain energy throughout treatment.

Circle 3 — Mind-Body

Mindfulness & Stress Relief

Guided breathing techniques, gentle visualization, and relaxation practices designed to lower stress and improve sleep quality.

Circle 4 — Community

Support Groups & Circles

Spiritual care counseling and peer-led support groups providing spaces for expression, connection, and navigating treatment uncertainty.

Circle 4 — Spiritual

Meaning & Resilience

Spiritual care support led by Ann Christensen, PhD, M.Div., open to all religious and non-religious affiliations to find comfort.

Resource Library

On-Demand Video Library

Easy access to class recordings for patients with transportation boundaries, severe fatigue, or schedule conflicts.

For Families

Supporting the person who supports the patient.
