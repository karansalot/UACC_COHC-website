Tom and Catherine Culley Circles of Holistic Care

Whole-person cancer support, every week.

A welcoming program for cancer center patients, survivors, caregivers, and families offering movement, nutrition, spiritual care, mindfulness, rehabilitation, and community connection.

Program Experience

Designed around what patients actually need next.

Weekly rhythm

Recurring movement classes make participation predictable and easy to remember.

Low-friction access

Patients can call, register interest, or ask their care team for help connecting.

Caregiver-inclusive

Caregivers can quickly see what is open to them and how to get support.

Integrated care

Movement, nutrition, spirituality, rehab, and support services are presented as one coordinated program.

Services

A broad circle of supportive care

Patients should see the full ecosystem of support without having to browse several disconnected pages.

Weekly movement

Yoga, Chair Yoga, Gentle Yoga, and Tai Chi

Accessible movement options for fatigue, balance, flexibility, sleep, stress reduction, and quality of life. Classes are beginner friendly and can be adapted for different mobility needs.

Nutrition

Nutrition During Treatment

Support for appetite changes, nausea, mouth sores, hydration, food safety, and maintaining strength through treatment.

Spiritual care

Meaning, purpose, and connection

Spiritual support can help patients and families navigate uncertainty, grief, anxiety, resilience, and personal values.

Mind-body

Mindfulness and Stress Reduction

Guided breathing, meditation, relaxation practices, and practical tools for coping with treatment and survivorship.

Rehabilitation

Cancer Rehabilitation

Support for weakness, pain, neuropathy, balance, swelling, speech, swallowing, memory, and return to daily activities.

Community

Support Groups and Drum Circles

Shared spaces for connection, expression, peer support, and community-building throughout the cancer journey.

Education

Survivorship and Wellness Education

Short programs and resources that help patients plan for life during and after treatment.

Access

Video Library

On-demand recordings for patients with transportation, immune, fatigue, or scheduling barriers.

Caregivers

Support for the person supporting the patient.

Caregivers need a clear place on the page too. This section makes their role visible and practical.

Caregivers can ask about

  • Which classes are open to caregivers and family members
  • How to attend when transportation is difficult
  • How to connect with spiritual care, social work, nutrition, or rehabilitation
  • How to prepare questions before an oncology visit

When symptoms change

If a patient is at home and concerned about symptoms, call sooner rather than later. For urgent or emergency symptoms, use emergency services.
